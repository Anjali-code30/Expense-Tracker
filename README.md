<h1>💰Expense Tracker</h1>

A simple and clean Expense Tracker web app to manage your daily income and expenses. 
Add transactions, see your balance update instantly, and never lose your data, everything is saved in your browser.

<h2>✨Features</h2>
<ul>
<li>➕ Add transactions: add income and expenses with a description and amount</li>
<li>🗑️ Delete transactions: remove any entry with one click</li>
<li>📊 Live tracking: total income, total expense and current balance update automatically</li>
<li>💾 Saved in localStorage: your data stays even after you refresh or close the browser</li>
<li>📱 Fully responsive: works smoothly on mobile, tablet and desktop</li>
</ul>

<h2>🛠️ Built With</h2>
<ul>
<li>HTML5: structure</li>
<li>CSS3: styling and responsive layout</li>
<li>JavaScript (ES6): logic, DOM manipulation and localStorage</li>
</ul>

<h3>📖 How to Use</h3>
<ul>
<li>Enter a description and the amount.</li>
<li>Choose (-)negative sign to add expense and add income normally.</li>
<li>You can see your current balance , expense and income.</li>
<li>Click the delete button on any transaction to remove it.</li>
</ul>
